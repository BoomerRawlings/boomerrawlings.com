THE BLACKBOOK / PAPER FIRST

Write your way forward.

Bring your black notebook and a pen. Nothing you write belongs on this screen.

Before day one

Where am I?

Describe your life financially, emotionally and physically. What do you do every day? What are you pretending to do? What are you putting off?

Where should I be?

What income, routine, identity and discipline do you want? Choose realistic standards.

What happened?

What decisions brought you here? What patterns repeat? Where did you undermine yourself? What did you stop doing?

Every fifteen days

In your notebook, answer: What brought me closer to the life I want? Where did I fall short? What will I adjust tomorrow?
