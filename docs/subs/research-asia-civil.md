# Asia / Oceania catalog research

Reviewed 2026-10-27. Result: **92 records** in `src/data/subs/asia.json`. Military classes, substantial named variants and unique physical prototypes; military rescue/special-purpose crewed craft included. No civilian catalog generated after scope confirmation.

## Evidence and attribution

- Historical Japan: [IJN class survey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Submarines_of_the_Imperial_Japanese_Navy), [Navypedia historical index](https://navypedia.org/ships/japan/jap_submarines.htm), [US Navy wartime shipbuilding report](https://www.history.navy.mil/research/library/online-reading-room/title-list-alphabetically/j/japanese-naval-shipbuilding.html). Individual prototype records use their dedicated design pages.
- Modern Japan: [MOD submarine evolution presentation](https://www.mod.go.jp/atla/research/dts2012/R2-2p.pdf), [JMSDF equipment](https://www.mod.go.jp/msdf/equipment/ships/index3.html), [Kawasaki rescue craft](https://www.khi.co.jp/mobility/marine/ships/submarine.html).
- China: [US Naval War College China Maritime Report 28](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/GOVPUB-D208-PURL-gpo245980/pdf/GOVPUB-D208-PURL-gpo245980.pdf); [research by Chinese submersible developers](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s11804-018-00062-6) supports Type 7103. Zhou is visibly qualified as a reported designation; no official Type 041 or settled propulsion claim.
- Korea: [NTI fleet research](https://www.nti.org/analysis/articles/north-korea-submarine-capabilities/), [ROK Foreign Ministry analysis of physical 2025 prototype](https://opendata.mofa.go.kr/mofapub/resource/Publication/14475.page), DAPA and Hanwha design material retained per row.
- Iran: [US Office of Naval Intelligence report](https://www.oni.navy.mil/Portals/12/Intel%20agencies/iran/Iran%20022217SP.pdf?ver=2017-02-28-082634-643), [NTI](https://www.nti.org/analysis/articles/iran-submarine-capabilities/).
- India/Taiwan: government commissioning/launch/trial announcements and Taiwan INDSR research retained per row.
- Collins: [Australian audit](https://www.anao.gov.au/sites/default/files/anao_report_1997-98_34.pdf) and [designer Saab/Kockums](https://www.saab.com/markets/australia/submarine/saabs-commitment-to-australia) establish Swedish design origin. Australian construction does not create a second origin.
- Kaigun-Holland Type 6 is a distinct US-origin export design completed in Japan; [JMSDF museum history](https://www.mod.go.jp/msdf/stc/museum_report_3.html) identifies the Holland drawings. Japanese Vickers-Kawasaki/Ha-6 is separately a Japanese experimental derivative.
- Country means design origin, not operator, builder, or every source of equipment. Ghadir, Yugo, Wuhan and other national derivatives retain their prior-design context in notes where relevant.

## Taxonomy and exclusions

- One class/design record, not every hull. Yu 1/1001/2001/3001 are grouped as the Army Type 3 transport family. Arihant includes enlarged S4/S4* follow-on boats. KSS-III includes Batch I/II. Kawasaki DSRV craft grouped as a design family. Most recurring production upgrades remain grouped.
- Historical Kaichu numbering differs among references; hull aliases disambiguate. Do not merge the 1960 and 1998 Oyashio designs.
- Imported/licensed Holland Type 1, British C/L, Fiat F1/F2, Schneider S, Type 209/214, Kilo, Scorpene, Agosta and ordinary Romeo/Golf remain represented under their original design countries by the other regional datasets, not duplicated as domestic Asian designs. Hangor (2024) is a named Chinese export derivative; it is not the French Daphne submarine named Hangor in 1971.
- Excluded paper-only/future proposals without verified physical construction: Indian Project 75 Alpha/S5, Chinese Types 095/096, Iranian Besat/Qaaem, South Korean KSS-500A/nuclear project, Taiwanese future batches, Australian Attack/SSN-AUKUS.
- Excluded research/tourist civilian submersibles, unmanned ROV/AUVs, diving bells and human torpedoes. U-Kanamono excluded: Navypedia describes a craft unable to fully submerge.
- No claim of exhaustiveness. Remaining review areas: obscure early Asian experiments; confidential current prototypes; incomplete public evidence on named Japanese rescue test craft (e.g. Chihiro); whether less-documented export modifications constitute separate designs; uncertain design contribution for licensed early classes. New evidence should expand this inventory rather than manufacture entries.
- Remora/Falcon and UK-designed LR7/DSAR rescue vehicles referred to the US/UK/Americas researcher to avoid operator-based duplication.

## Validation

JSON parsed; 92 nonempty type/country/source rows; unique type-country keys; all source URLs absolute HTTPS. Links are provenance, not per-vessel navigation. Primary institution/builder records preferred; historical specialist indexes and general class lists used for names and coverage cross-checks. Some historical prototype pages required direct HTTP retrieval after the web reader timed out.

Final integration: Nagapasa / DSME1400 added as a Korean export redesign of German Type209, with Indonesian Navy and Defence Ministry evidence. Dataset now92 records/48 URLs; see CATALOG.md.
