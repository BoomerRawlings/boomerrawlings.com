# Continental European submarine catalogue research

Checked 2026-10-27. Dataset: `src/data/subs/europe.json` (315 records). Country means design/programme origin, including documented joint development; it is not an operator list. Foreign-built or imported classes retain their designers' origin. Classes and distinct prototypes are counted once; sister boats and straightforward transfer names are omitted.

## Scope and evidence

Military classes, unique prototypes, and military rescue craft that reached physical construction. Includes incomplete hulls and physical prototype programmes. Excludes civilian vessels and paper-only designs. Every record has source URLs; the UI can expose their deduplicated raw hyperlinks separately.

- Historic class discovery/cross-check: national submarine lists for [France](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_submarines_of_France), [Italy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_submarines_of_Italy), [Netherlands](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_submarines_of_the_Netherlands), [Sweden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_submarines_of_the_Swedish_Navy), and [Austria-Hungary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Austro-Hungarian_U-boat_classes). These are secondary indexes, not evidence that every class was designed by its operator.
- German historical type coverage: [WWI U-boat types](https://uboat.net/wwi/types/), [WWII types](https://uboat.net/types/), [midgets](https://uboat.net/types/midget.htm), and [project histories](https://uboat.net/types/projects.htm). Separate physical late-war developments from projects that never started.
- Origin corrections: the [Dutch military museum's historical journal](https://assets.nmm.nl/app/uploads/2022/10/Jaargang-2-nummer-1.pdf) attributes O 6 to Electric Boat and O 7 to Denny-Hay; [Whitehead's Ægir design](https://www.navypedia.org/ships/denmark/dk_ss_aegir.htm) is Austro-Hungarian despite Danish construction. Finnish and Romanian IvS boats are German designs despite the firm's Dutch registration. Licensed Swedish derivatives identify their documented German/Italian partnership.
- Modern construction: [OCCAR U212 NFS keel laying](https://occar.int/news/first-u212-nfs-submarine-keel-laying-ceremony), [TechnicAtome SNLE 3G milestones](https://www.technicatome.com/realisations/snle-3g/), [Navantia S80](https://www.navantia.es/en/product/s80/), [TKMS Type 218SG](https://www.tkmsgroup.com/news/article/tkms-and-stengineering-sign-mou-in-singapore), and [French procurement industrial review](https://www.defense.gouv.fr/sites/default/files/dga/Industrial%20Calepin%202025%20English%20version.pdf) support current labels and physical construction, including Dakar.
- Turkey: [STM](https://www.stm.com.tr/en/press-releases/first-stage-stm500-test-production-pressure-hull-begins) confirms the STM500 pressure-hull prototype; [Ministry of Defence](https://www.msb.gov.tr/SlaytHaber/94c5e0a1b2ed42ef9cb76078e3dea700) confirms MİLDEN test-block construction. These entries do not imply completed vessels.
- Special cases: [French historical archives](https://www.servicehistorique.sga.defense.gouv.fr/sites/default/files/2020-02/SHDAA_REP_5I_NO_661_1773_1976.pdf) document Q-244 hull construction and reuse for Gymnote; [Swedish armed forces](https://www.forsvarsmakten.se/siteassets/5-organisation/forsta-ubatsflj/forsta-ubatsflottiljen/ubatsraddningsfarkost.pdf) document URF. [US Naval Institute](https://www.usni.org/magazines/proceedings/2001/february/nautilus-whats-name) confirms French-funded Nautilus (1800), its military purpose, and French construction; designer citizenship is retained only in the internal note.

## Limits and exclusions

This is broad sourced coverage, not a demonstrated exhaustive inventory. Small clandestine/experimental designs and the boundary between classes, subtypes, licensed derivatives, and major rebuilds remain the main completeness risks. Generic Type 209 covers its displacement variants; many operator-specific names are aliases rather than separate rows. SX-756 is sparsely documented and is supported by a specialist secondary source, not a public shipyard archive.

Excluded: Orka/Blacksword Barracuda (the [Dutch defence ministry](https://www.defensie.nl/nieuws/20260616-f21-torpedo-onderdeel-van-toekomstige-orka-klasse) still scheduled first steel for 2027); Type 216 and other paper concepts; never-started French Phénix/Émeraude projects. Type XVIII and XVIIK have conflicting secondary claims of construction; omitted conservatively because [uboat.net's U-797 record](https://www.uboat.net/boats/nc_797.html) explicitly says it was never laid down. Further archival evidence could resolve these disputed cases. Crew-exposed torpedo vehicles are not catalogued as submarine classes.

No verbatim source passages copied into the dataset. Names and country assignments are factual labels; notes document classification decisions.
