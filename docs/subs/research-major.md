# SUBS inventory: Americas, Britain, Russia

Researched 2026-10-27. `src/data/subs/major.json`: 252 source-backed class/design/prototype records before cross-region deduplication. Country denotes design origin, not the operator or every construction location. Shared design lineages retain both origins; historical Russian Empire/Soviet Union labels remain distinct. Drebbel's English prototypes use England.

## Coverage and evidence

- US Navy class inventory: [national historical list](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_submarine_classes_of_the_United_States_Navy), with article links extracted from that inspected index. Includes unique naval experimental boats, nuclear and conventional classes, and Columbia under construction. Current attack classes checked against [US Navy fact file](https://www.navy.mil/Resources/Fact-Files/Display-FactFiles/Article/2169558/attack-submarines-ssn/).
- Early American military prototypes: [NHHC Turtle history](https://www.history.navy.mil/research/histories/ship-histories/danfs/t/turtle-i.html), [NHHC Intelligent Whale](https://www.history.navy.mil/research/histories/ship-histories/danfs/i/intelligent-whale-sub.html), and [Hunley museum's Pioneer/American Diver history](https://www.hunley.org/naval-innovation/). Holland I–V individual histories inspected. Civilian Argonaut/Alvin omitted.
- British classes: [Royal Navy historical index](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_submarine_classes_of_the_Royal_Navy), including unique Nautilus, Swordfish, X1, rescue craft, and [Dreadnought SSBN construction](https://www.royalnavy.mod.uk/equipment/submarine/dreadnought-class). S (1914) and W retain Italian/French design participation; Holland/H retain American origin. [Drebbel's prototypes](https://utmsi.utexas.edu/science-and-the-sea/radio-program/first-submarine/) were developed in England and tested by the Royal Navy.
- Imperial/Soviet/Russian classes: [historical index](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Soviet_and_Russian_submarine_classes), crosschecked with the [RussianShips project register](https://russianships.info/eng/submarines/). Individual register pages establish actual construction of Projects 648, 664 and 945AB despite cancellation. Modern conversions, ballistic/cruise-missile classes, military deep-submergence and rescue designs included. [Sevmash annual report](https://www.sevmash.ru/download/GO%20Sevmash_2017.pdf) corroborates 885M and 09852; [Admiralty Shipyards](https://admship.ru/press/news/ao-admiralteyskie-verfi-zalozhili-avtonomnyy-glubokovodnyy-apparat-sergey-bavilin/) confirms Project 18200 construction.
- Early Russian prototypes: [Central Naval Museum](https://eng.navalmuseum.ru/filials/baltic_fleet/4), its [Dzhevetsky collection record](https://special.navalmuseum.ru/collection/ship/modeli?id=1210), [Nikonov exhibition](https://navalmuseum.ru/filials/narodovolec/news?id=2771), and [Russian naval archival guide](https://rgakfd.ru/funds/145/stroitelstvo-podvodnyh-lodok). [Admiralty Shipyards' own historical register](https://admship.ru/production/archive/spravochnik-proektov-podvodnykh-lodok-postroennykh-na-admiralteyskikh-verfyakh/proekt-ostekhbyuro/) establishes physical APSS and Pygmy prototypes.
- Military submersibles: [NHHC Mystic](https://www.history.navy.mil/content/history/museums/nmusn/explore/photography/underwater-search-and-recovery-equipment/mystic-dsrv-1.html), [SEAL Museum SDVs](https://www.navysealmuseum.org/exhibits/seal-delivery-vehicles-sdv-manned-submersibles-for-special-operations), [GAO ASDS](https://www.gao.gov/assets/a261269.html), and [Lockheed Martin DCS delivery](https://news.lockheedmartin.com/2023-07-24-US-SOCOM-Declares-Initial-Operational-Capability-for-Lockheed-Martins-New-Dry-Combat-Submersible). Canadian Remora and Canadian/US Falcon derive from [OceanWorks' design record](https://oceanworks.com/our-business/military/military-project-samples/), with [Australian government corroboration](https://www.anao.gov.au/work/performance-audit/management-collins-class-operations-sustainment).
- Latin America: [Chilean Navy Flach history](https://www.armada.cl/noticias-navales/armada-inicio-busqueda-del-submarino-flach), [Colombian Navy regional history including Ecuador's Hipopotamo](https://ventanillavirtual.armada.mil.co/sites/default/files/fuerza_submarina_2.pdf), [Brazilian Navy Tikuna history](https://www.marinha.mil.br/prosub/sites/www.marinha.mil.br.prosub/files/livro_submarino.pdf), [Brazilian defense-industry Riachuelo record](https://www.gov.br/mme/pt-br/assuntos/orgaos-vinculados/nuclep/produtos-e-servicos/setor-de-defesa), and inspected Toro Submarino history. Ordinary imported classes are represented by their originating designs elsewhere.

## Boundaries and remaining uncertainty

- Military classes, unique prototypes and actual construction only. Civilian/tourist vehicles, narco craft and paper-only AUKUS/SSN(X)/other proposals excluded. Noncombat military rescue, special-purpose and experimental vessels included. Individual hulls within a class are not additional records.
- Cuban Delfin origin is disputed: [USNI analysis](https://news.usni.org/2021/05/17/new-photos-reveal-details-of-cubas-tiny-lethal-attack-submarine) describes a Cuban design; [Navypedia](https://navypedia.org/ships/cuba/cub_ss_delfin.htm) attributes a North Korean design. Visible country explicitly says `Cuba (disputed)`.
- Alvaro Alberto omitted pending clear evidence of actual hull construction: [Brazilian Navy status report](https://www.marinha.mil.br/sites/default/files/transp-e-prest-contas/item%205.1.7.1-2025-1.pdf) discusses qualification/preliminary section work and contracting. This inventory does not equate a planned program with a built boat.
- No exhaustive historical claim: secret projects, poorly documented early prototypes and boundaries between redesigns/conversions/subclasses can change the total. National indexes are secondary evidence for much of the historical naming inventory, supplemented by primary records above. Bibliography URLs were inspected directly, in search excerpts, or extracted as links from inspected national indexes; no invented article URLs.
- Cross-region review needed for licensed/export descendants (Holland, RN H, Som), jointly designed early European craft, and major modern conversions. Other regional files own German-origin Karp/Forelle, Nordenfelt variants, and Fulton Nautilus.

Final integration: Matros Pyotr Koshka added with inspected Navypedia construction evidence; see CATALOG.md. Dataset now252 records/189 URLs. Keta explicitly excluded as a semi-submersible surface craft.
