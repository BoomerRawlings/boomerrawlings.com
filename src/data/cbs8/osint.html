About the grades, sources and review limits

Rating key

The 102 documented rows comprise 97 A–D ratings and 5 utility assessments. Another 326 rows have only a provisional lead-only classification; 463 remain unverified; 286 total are not sources of connection evidence. No percentage-accuracy claims are made.

Documented means provider documentation, an originating record portal, a documented maintenance notice or a regulatory source was reviewed. A provider claim is not an independent benchmark. Provisional uses saved bookmark metadata only.

Unverified includes 34 unresolved retrieval attempts and 429 entries not independently assessed. A failed reader does not establish that a service is dead, unsafe or fraudulent. N/A is not a security, privacy or performance endorsement.

No paid reports, searches about real people, face uploads, software execution or breach-data downloads were used. All grades apply only to the stated scope.

How purpose and grade differ

Purpose explains what you would use the resource for. A description of its advertised function does not prove that it works today or produces accurate results.

Grade is the original audit's assessment of a specific type of evidence, not an overall score for the product. These are editorial categories, not ratings issued by providers or an external standards body. Sorting puts A–D first, followed by provisional C-P, unverified U, and N/A.

The Berkeley Protocol provides a wider framework for collecting, preserving, verifying, and analysing digital open-source information. The letter grades here are not part of that protocol.

The Excel download preserves the supplied audit. This web edition adds purpose descriptions and separately cited source notes without silently rewriting its grades.

Scope and provenance
