These experiments include *The Silent Index*, a Cicada 3301-inspired sealed puzzle generated by AI and built to be AI-proof. Its future stages remain encrypted until current proofs are solved and authenticated. *The Unrendered World* is a low-poly procedural environment with shifting distant geometry and a fixed seed that makes its architecture repeatable.

They are designed around dependable hidden rules: authenticated proofs govern encrypted progression, deterministic generation makes worlds reproducible, and measurable state changes and reversible iteration keep experiments controlled as they evolve.
