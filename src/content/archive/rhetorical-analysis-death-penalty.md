> Rhetorical analysis produced for ENGL C1001 at Southwestern College on December 8, 2025. The original paper is embedded below.

This essay compares a Federalist Society discussion supporting capital punishment with an Amnesty International brief opposing it. The analysis focuses on how each source uses authority, evidence, emotion, tone, and format to reach its audience.

The paper concludes that Amnesty International makes the more effective rhetorical case because its shorter structure and more moderated emotional appeal leave more room for undecided readers. This is an analysis of persuasion, not a general review of death-penalty policy or evidence.
