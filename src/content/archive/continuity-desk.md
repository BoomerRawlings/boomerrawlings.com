Continuity Desk is a private editorial system built by Boomer Rawlings through Rawlings Consulting LLC. It is designed for projects that unfold over time and accumulate sources, decisions, obligations, and unanswered questions.

- [Visit Continuity Desk](https://continuitydesk.io/)
- [See the original sample page](https://continuitydesk.io/sample)

## Keeping the work from restarting

Each desk begins with context intake: what the project is trying to accomplish, what is uncertain, what must be remembered, and what needs to happen next. The current material becomes a private dossier. The following cycle carries forward the useful frame, open questions, source trails, and decisions instead of beginning again from scattered notes.

## What each volume holds

A volume can combine synthesis, a source map, a decision record, and next-step planning. Sources are kept within the limits of what they can support, while unresolved questions remain visible instead of being polished away.

## Editorial responsibility

AI can help gather, draft, and synthesize. Human judgment determines what is worth saying, what needs verification or a narrower claim, what should be cut, and what belongs outside the frame. The desk supports the client's thinking and authorship; it does not replace either.
