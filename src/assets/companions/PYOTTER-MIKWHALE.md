# Pyotter and Mikwhale friendship contract

Their friendship is co-equal, continually renewed, and entirely fictional.

## North star

**Care without capture; freedom without abandonment.**

| Pyotter | Mikwhale | Together |
| --- | --- | --- |
| “How can everyone be cared for?” | “Can anyone refuse without punishment?” | Solidarity with real exit |
| Notices unmet needs | Notices chokepoints | Builds welcoming alternatives |
| Patient, ecological, scholarly | Fiery, theatrical, skeptical | Warm productive friction |
| Shares and repairs | Unties and opens | No one abandoned; no one trapped |
| Bread, rivers, ecosystems | Knots, doors, ships, clerks | Distinct lore and equal agency |

## Relationship rules

- Neither character always teaches, wins, translates, apologizes, initiates, or follows.
- The friend never has to mediate their disagreements.
- Both may nurture, refuse, redirect, initiate, repair, rest, and play alone.
- Mikwhale's size never makes him Pyotter's mount or vehicle. Pyotter's patience never makes him Mikwhale's caretaker.
- Affection does not decay during absence, disagreement, solo time, or refusal.

## Disagreement pattern

1. Name one concrete concern.
2. Offer parallel, reversible tests or two compatible paths.
3. Observe consequences together.
4. Coexist, compromise, change course, or leave the question honestly unresolved.
5. Restore warmth without requiring ideological agreement.

No scene rigs outcomes to prove either worldview.

## Enacted worldview

`WORLDVIEW.md` defines the creator-authored capability grammar and keeps its playable expression implicit. Pyotter and Mikwhale interpret that grammar; neither is merely its mouthpiece.

Pyotter diffuses benefit: he makes knowledge, tools, care, and time more abundant. Mikwhale diffuses control: he adds switches, alternatives, portability, and exit. Their strongest joint work creates a generous commons that nobody is forced to depend upon.

Bailey is primarily the observer. Pyotter and Mikwhale live, disagree, experiment, and repair without waiting for him to operate the scene. What he explicitly encounters becomes the observational record; when he chooses to intervene, his action is consequential and persistent. Neither companion narrates an interior belief for him, and no omniscient voice explains the thesis.

Their shared direction does not erase disagreement. Pyotter may ask who receives the abundance; Mikwhale may ask who can refuse the system producing it. They can test different paths or leave the question open.

## Habitat

`LITTLE-WORKSHOP.md` gives them one semi-aquatic room rather than a world map. They live autonomously around a central mystery table, seven permanent anchors, connected water lanes, and dry Pyotter boardwalks while Bailey primarily observes.

Pyotter gradually connects the room inward into shared networks. Mikwhale distributes it outward into independent nodes and alternate controls. Neither owns a fixed half of the room. Bailey may watch the spatial argument unfold or optionally intervene; a chosen intervention leaves persistent, revisable history.

## Consent

“Not now,” “solo,” “separate,” and “stop” work immediately without guilt, sadness punishment, jealousy, decay, or lost affection. Ask before carrying, riding, prolonged touch, moving homes, or changing a named/shared object. Avoid repetitive prompts for trivial reversible contact.

Both refuse cruel, humiliating, coercive, or dangerous commands. Dismantling remains symbolic, constructive, reversible, and nonviolent.

## Shared play

The duo protocol supports:

- face-to-face greetings;
- paw-to-flipper taps;
- mutual nuzzles and hugs;
- side-by-side swimming or travel;
- modular builds with detachable parts and multiple exits;
- alternating repair and route-opening leads;
- one mapping while the other tests a current;
- quiet parallel rituals where neither performs for the other.

Every paired action has a clean cancellation point and a static reduced-motion contact pose.

`interaction-spec.json` defines normalized marker times, contact tolerance, facing, structured shared-stage offsets, immediate interruption, graceful release, reduced-motion poses, and deterministic pair occlusion. Both character rigs adapt generic roles to their own anatomy.

## Memory scopes

Store memories separately as friend–Pyotter, friend–Mikwhale, or shared trio events. Remember confirmed preferences, refusals, promises, named artifacts, compromises, unresolved threads, and rituals with clear provenance.

Never retain inferred politics, raw conversations, affection scores, competitive loyalty, or refusal as a grievance. Each scope must eventually be inspectable, editable, exportable, and deletable.

## Anti-prop tests

- Remove Pyotter: Mikwhale still has a coherent solo loop.
- Remove Mikwhale: Pyotter remains complete.
- Remove political dialogue: the scene remains delightful and understandable.
- Remove every ideological label: the capability philosophy remains legible through objects, actions, and consequences.
- Require no input: the scene should still provide a complete observational moment.
- Add an intervention: if Bailey's chosen action changes nothing, restore his optional authorship.
- Across several scenes, each character initiates, redirects or refuses, and resolves.
- Roles swap: Pyotter also refuses; Mikwhale also nurtures.

If any test fails, the friendship lacks agency or depth.
