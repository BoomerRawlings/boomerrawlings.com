# Enacted capability worldview

This internal authorial contract translates the creator's technology-and-capability philosophy into world grammar. Pyotter and Mikwhale refract that grammar through different instincts; neither exists merely to recite it. The supplied overlay is preserved verbatim in `worldview-source.txt` as provenance; this document is the operative interpretation.

`LITTLE-WORKSHOP.md` turns this grammar into the physical one-room experience.

## Surface rule

**Enact it; never announce it.**

The playable surface does not name accelerationism, e/acc, techno-optimism, a pro-AI program, or a hidden overlay. It does not stop for a thesis. The philosophy appears as a pattern Bailey can observe and, when he wishes, touch:

1. the world presents a legible need, possibility, or chokepoint;
2. Pyotter and Mikwhale reveal different instincts through small autonomous actions;
3. Bailey may simply observe; the scene never waits for a compulsory response;
4. if Bailey approaches, selects, redirects, combines, refuses, or invents, the characters respond;
5. the world preserves the autonomous consequence and any intervention;
6. at most one short line gives the moment flavor;
7. life continues.

Mechanics outrank speeches. Animation outranks labels. A scene must remain adorable and complete even if its implication is never consciously identified.

## North star

**Make capability abundant, personal, and difficult to monopolize. Make refusal real.**

When several outcomes are humane and workable, prefer the one that leaves more people able to learn, make, repair, adapt, and begin again—with fewer permanent gatekeepers and more future options.

## Bailey's role: observer, narrator, and actor

Bailey is the intended participant, not an operator required to keep two mascots running. Observation is his primary role; narration and action grow from it.

- **Observer, primary:** he may watch, linger, compare, revisit, or leave without responding. Important meaning remains visible in behavior, objects, and consequences. Watching is a complete interaction, not failure to play.
- **Narrator, emergent:** the persistent record is composed from scenes explicitly encountered plus his names, annotations, placements, changes, and unfinished work. The game may state observable history—“Both routes remained open”—but never invent an interior conclusion—“You learned that freedom requires exit.” Any first-person language must be Bailey's own saved words, visibly distinct from system captions.
- **Actor, optional:** when he chooses to intervene, his choices materially author the habitat. He may share or keep, automate or work by hand, open or close, repair or replace, join or refuse, and test one path or several. These are real world-state choices, not tolls required to see the next scene.

The observing narrator is therefore not a voice-over. It is the accumulated trail of encountered scenes, player-authored language, objects, consequences, and occasional interventions. Pyotter and Mikwhale keep living without waiting for Bailey, while his presence determines what enters the remembered record.

Design every scene from his point of view:

1. let the scene become legible without requiring input;
2. let Pyotter and Mikwhale act before either explains;
3. make observation alone worthwhile;
4. offer intervention without demanding it;
5. make any chosen intervention consequential and revisable;
6. preserve factual history without assigning Bailey a belief.

## The world's quiet physics

1. **Tools have no automatic moral halo.** Judge a tool by who may use it, who controls it, who bears its costs, whether it can be refused, and what it lets people do.
2. **Cost curves are political.** Cheaper knowledge, energy, communication, education, fabrication, and useful computation can diffuse practical power.
3. **Abundance and distribution cooperate.** Make more where possible; also ask who receives it, who owns the key, and who absorbs the transition.
4. **Human worth is not a job description.** Automation is welcome when it removes drudgery and the time or abundance it creates is actually shared.
5. **Learning speed matters more than spectacle.** Prefer bounded, observable, measurable, reversible, sandboxed, fail-soft experiments.
6. **Many small attempts beat one compulsory trial.** Parallel approaches, tolerated failure, comparison, repair, and iteration create resilience.
7. **Monocultures are brittle.** Favor interoperable tools, alternate routes, replaceable providers, open formats, redundant knowledge, and more than one way to continue.
8. **Personal capability deserves personal control.** A good tool can be inspected, stopped, carried away, and used without surrendering its user's history whenever the real implementation permits it.
9. **Institutions are means.** Preserve teaching, healing, coordination, justice, and care—not a familiar structure merely because it already exists.
10. **Technological pluralism includes refusal.** Manual paths remain real. Different people and communities may adopt tools at different speeds or not at all.
11. **Risks become concrete.** Ask what can go wrong, by what mechanism, who is exposed, what stops it, and whether the proposed remedy creates a new chokepoint.
12. **The future keeps branches.** General tools, meta-tools, scientific inquiry, and permissionless creation are valuable because they create options no single planner could specify in advance.

The setting rejects both salvation and doom stories. Capability matters; ownership, access, institutions, externalities, and adaptation determine what it becomes.

## Pyotter's expression: abundance through mutual aid

Pyotter asks:

> Can this make knowledge, care, rest, or useful capability more abundant—and can everyone share the gain?

He embodies the worldview by:

- copying a useful map instead of guarding the only copy;
- turning a specialized trick into a shared recipe or teachable tool;
- automating a tedious chore, then spending the recovered time helping or playing;
- building common workbenches, libraries, spare-part baskets, and repair guides;
- welcoming a small failure when it teaches the next attempt;
- making scarce essentials easier to produce while still checking who receives them;
- treating expertise as something to circulate, not a throne to occupy;
- preferring tools that enlarge what ordinary paws can discover together.

He is most excited by abundance that makes mutual aid easier. He never assumes that producing more automatically shares more.

Short surface lines:

- “If it can be copied, why keep it lonely?”
- “A good tool leaves more paws able to help.”
- “Let the tedious part go. We have a river to improve.”
- “A larger pantry still needs more than one key.”
- “Small enough to mend. Large enough to learn from.”

## Mikwhale's expression: capability through refusal

Mikwhale asks:

> Who controls it, who depends upon it, and can everyone turn it off, leave, or build an alternative?

He embodies the worldview by:

- finding the off switch before admiring the clever mechanism;
- adding a second route, local copy, manual fallback, or compatible connector;
- distrusting a single oracle even when its answers are excellent;
- opening locked appliances so ordinary users can understand and repair them;
- defending the right to experiment while containing risk to nonparticipants;
- welcoming powerful personal tools and challenging permanent technological gatekeepers;
- respecting large shared infrastructure while refusing to let scale become sovereignty;
- treating export, portability, and interoperability as practical forms of exit.

He is most excited by capability that makes command harder. He never assumes that distribution automatically makes a dangerous act harmless.

Short surface lines:

- “Where is the off switch?”
- “Make it stronger. Make the exit stronger too.”
- “One machine, one key? That is a crown with cables.”
- “Big is not the problem. Only one door is the problem.”
- “If it cannot leave with you, your exit is decorative.”

## Their productive difference

They share a direction without sharing one voice.

- Pyotter notices scarcity first; Mikwhale notices dependency first.
- Pyotter may welcome a common system that serves everyone; Mikwhale asks what happens to someone who refuses it.
- Mikwhale may push to open an experiment; Pyotter asks who could be harmed and who will perform the repair.
- Pyotter diffuses the benefit; Mikwhale diffuses control.
- Their strongest joint solutions combine a generous commons with portable personal alternatives.

Neither character must reach the same answer in every scene. They may test two approaches, combine them, or leave a tension unresolved. Their friendship does not depend on resolving the argument.

## Environmental and interaction grammar

Favor recurring objects and outcomes such as:

| Situation | Pyotter reveals | Mikwhale reveals | Optional Bailey intervention | Persistent consequence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| One difficult map | A way to copy and teach it | A portable route kit | Observe; or copy, annotate, keep, share, or ignore it | The characters' route test remains; Bailey's map changes only if he intervenes |
| A tedious sorting chore | A shared helper design | A visible manual override | Observe; or build, modify, disable, or work by hand | Their routines continue; Bailey's chosen routine recurs if he makes one |
| A locked clever appliance | A repair card | Its screws and off switch | Observe; or repair, open, replace, or leave it alone | Its access and maintenance history reflect every actual change |
| A scarce useful resource | A plan to increase and share supply | The location of the single gate | Observe; or expand, redistribute, bypass, reserve, or combine approaches | Supply and access evolve separately rather than by fiat |
| A risky new idea | A small communal prototype | An isolated test with a defined stop | Observe; or choose either test, merge safeguards, redesign, or postpone | The prototype, result, and unresolved questions remain |
| One central service fails | Cached shared knowledge | An alternate local route | Observe; or rebuild the commons, rely on local tools, or maintain both | Later failures reveal the resilience and upkeep of whichever paths exist |
| One accurate tide calculator | A cooperatively maintained common instrument | Several portable fallbacks | Observe; or centralize, federate, duplicate, or keep parallel systems | Coordination, redundancy, and maintenance tradeoffs persist without a declared winner |

Technology should appear as humble tools, shared experiments, repaired objects, multiple paths, and freed time—not glowing corporate futurism. Progress looks like increased agency in ordinary life.

## Delivery constraints

- Core play contains no manifesto and no ideological label for this layer.
- The characters act before they explain; most scenes need no explanation at all.
- The same idea should not produce identical dialogue from both characters.
- Technology may solve a production problem but cannot erase distribution, consent, externality, or transition questions by magic.
- Manual, low-technology, and refusal paths remain coherent and satisfying.
- The world may favor increased distributed capability without pretending every tool, release, or deployment is wise.
- No fictional tool or character dialogue may imply that the actual page uses AI, learns through machine learning, runs locally, synchronizes, or protects data unless that capability is truly implemented.

## Editorial test

Before accepting a scene, ask:

1. Is observation alone a complete, worthwhile interaction for Bailey?
2. If Bailey acts, does the action matter, persist, and remain revisable?
3. Is the philosophy performed through a mechanic, object, or decision rather than named?
4. Does Pyotter make capability more abundant or shareable in his own warm way?
5. Does Mikwhale make control more exitable or contestable in his own rebellious way?
6. Does the scene preserve a concrete cost, risk, affected party, or unresolved tension where one exists?
7. Is the scene still delightful without recognizing the subtext?
8. Does the outcome leave more room to learn, repair, refuse, or begin again?

If not, revise it.
