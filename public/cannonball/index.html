Submission criteria

Record your run

  1. Start at North Point. Finish at Gilman Gateway.

    Use the two landmarks on the course card. Choose any legal route. Yield to people around you.

  2. Record one continuous GPS activity.

    Use Strava or a similar tracker. The reviewer needs timestamped GPS data showing both endpoints and your full elapsed time, including stops.

  3. Send your time and proof.

    Choose a public handle. Use Motorized if you had any motor assistance during the run. Use Human-powered for running, cycling, skating, or other unpowered travel. Enter your elapsed time and the date and time you finished in Pacific time. Include a reviewer-accessible activity link and any optional notes.

Submission privacy

Your route stays with the reviewer.

We keep your GPS link, route, notes, and personal information private. We publish your chosen handle, division, elapsed time, and finish date and time after verification.

Your activity app controls who can see the original recording. Use its limited-access sharing option when available and make sure the reviewer can open the link.

Where it started

The original Cannonball

In 1971, Brock Yates and a handful of other drivers set out from New York for Redondo Beach, California. Word of the unsanctioned coast-to-coast run spread among drivers who thought they could beat the clock.

Our version fits between two campus landmarks. Part of the fun is finding a line of your own and seeing how fast you can cover it safely. Know a friend who could beat the best? Tell them where the clock starts.
