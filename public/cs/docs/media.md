# Media acquisition and attribution

Player portraits and team marks are editorial identity aids, not statistical evidence. They do not imply the image or logo existed at the displayed historical date, or endorsement by any player/organization.

`config/media-selection.json` explicitly selects Wikimedia Commons file titles and verified canonical player IDs. `config/media-official.json` separately documents official brand/press resources; these are never labeled Creative Commons. No name-based automatic player matching occurs. `node src/acquire-media.mjs` retrieves Commons metadata, checks explicit CC BY/CC BY-SA/public-domain declarations, caches originals under `data/raw/media/originals`, and writes web assets plus `config/media.json`. SHA-256 checksums, authors, license links, source pages, source timestamps and modifications are preserved. HTTP 429 is recorded without immediate retry; later resumptions use the cache.

`config/media-lock.json` pins original and display SHA-256 checksums. Changed remote bytes fail validation and require a documented source-revision review before updating the lock. A rebuild cannot silently discard any locked asset or overwrite the manifest with a reduced collection. `node src/acquire-media.mjs --offline --output work/media-cache-check.json` validates a cached rebuild without network access or authoritative manifest replacement. `node --test tests/media-rebuild.test.mjs` checks that official marks, display-only associations, license fields and checksums survive.

Existing source crops and source-provided thumbnails are used. No faces are synthesized and no local bitmap editing occurs. Browser `object-fit` may crop the visible framing. The media-credits interface must expose attribution, source and license for every asset.

The 13 requested historic validation players have licensed portraits; additional available portraits extend coverage. Missing players receive a labeled initials/silhouette fallback. Portrait availability never controls analytical eligibility.

Thirty-eight organization marks were acquired from Commons with explicit reuse status. Six more come from official resources: [G2 media guidelines](https://g2esports.com/pages/brand-guidelines), [Fnatic press/brand kit](https://fnatic.com/brand), [Liquid brand kit](https://teamliquid.com/brand-kit), [NIP external brand resources](https://nip.gl/pages/brand), [Virtus.pro's copying policy](https://virtus.pro/), and [Sinners' public press kit](https://www.sinners.gg/en/presskit). Virtus.pro requires an active source link. The brand kits are restricted editorial brand usage, with copyright retained, not open licenses. G2 explicitly addresses media use; Fnatic/Liquid/NIP/Sinners selection interprets their published press/brand resources according to their stated purpose. This distinction and the exact basis are recorded per asset. Liquid's linked full Figma guide could not be retrieved; its original crest-on-dark variant is preserved unmodified. Official assets require proportion-preserving, full-opacity rendering without filters or crest effects; NIP requires clear space of at least one-third of its symbol size, also conservatively applied to Sinners.

A [Fnatic Commons upload was deleted for copyright](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/User_talk:Artemis_Wu#File:FNATIC_LOGO.png); the replacement here comes directly from Fnatic's own published press kit under its separate usage basis. Vitality, FURIA, Falcons and Luminosity remain text fallbacks: no suitable editorial permission acquired. [Vitality's terms](https://vitality.gg/pages/conditions-generales-dutilisation-1) require prior written permission for reproducing site elements. Commons search also returned unrelated entities such as the Belgian television production “Team Spirit” and Swiss Dignitas association; these were rejected. The selected Spirit dragon is an officially released CC BY-SA asset; Dignitas's selected legacy esports mark has a confirmed Wikimedia permission ticket.

`config/media-display-identities.json` associates nine Ektarr IDs with already licensed portraits using source full legal name, alias, country and manually checked HLTV profile identity. The media pipeline itself never mutates canonical IDs, analytical windows or cohorts. A separate statistical identity review promoted these nine links plus b1t, sh1ro, KSCERATO, degster and w0nderful through `config/identity-upgrade-reviewed.json` and an atomic migration into `config/identity-anchors.json`; protected counts and performance values were unchanged. No defensible portrait license was acquired for those five additional players. sh1ro's discovered Commons upload lacked a license; w0nderful's purported CC0 image credited an unknown news photographer without evidence of a rights-holder release. Both were rejected. There are 24 distinct portraits, 33 portrait lookup keys, and 44 logos after this pass.

Assets retain their individual licenses. Public-domain status concerns copyright only; trademarks remain with their owners. Licensed portrait availability, older jerseys and logo-version differences are explicitly disclosed. Organization display-name aliases in the media manifest are visual lookup hints; they do not merge analytical team identities or roster lineages.

The expanded pass added LDLC, London Conspiracy, mTw, Complexity, FlipSid3, MIBR, Ghost, OpTic, TSM, HellRaisers, Immortals, NRG, 100 Thieves, 9z, Imperial, BetBoom, Endpoint, CLG, Heroic, Gen.G, paiN, BIG, ECLOT, Bad News Eagles, FunPlus Phoenix, Invictus, Giants and Sinners. Selection prioritized teams present in actual historical and active top-25 exports. Additional academy/team-name aliases reuse the same organization's mark without claiming the logo is contemporary. ENCE's indexed media-kit page redirected to a password-gated shop; no bypass attempted. Unrelated search results, unsupported complex-logo uploads and a Riyadh Falcons Call of Duty franchise mark were rejected.

`web/team-branding.js` documents each palette against source-art colors or explicit brand resources. Reusable transparent marks may be displayed through CSS alpha masks; BIG's CC BY 3.0 white-on-dark photograph and Ghost's public-domain white-on-black mark use CSS screen blending. These display adaptations preserve original source bytes and attribution, and retain each source license. Official restricted marks never receive these effects. Opaque white-backed Dignitas, Endpoint and ECLOT marks remain visible in Media credits but use typography in bars. No asset is redrawn or automatically background-removed. Local discovery metadata and the per-asset review are retained under `data/raw/media/discovery/`; checksums are pinned for all 68 unique images.

Final targeted check on 2026-11-27 found no explicit media reuse grant for the remaining three prominent marks. [Vitality's press page](https://vitality.gg/en-uk/blogs/presse) and [licensing page](https://vitality.gg/en-uk/pages/licensing) offer inquiry forms; its terms still require written permission. [FURIA's website terms](https://us.furia.gg/terms), updated 2026-04-29, restrict republication and reject an inferred license. Their logos were not acquired. Search results for the Vitality health-insurance newsroom and Atlanta Falcons brand playbook were unrelated entities and rejected. No permission request was sent.
