## Photographs and marks

The public app's Media credits and `data/meta.json` identify each asset's source, author, license and modification status. There are 24 distinct licensed photographs and 16 organization marks; shared identity lookup entries do not create additional assets. All 40 assets have source/license links, author attribution and SHA-256 records.

Commons photographs and marks retain their individual CC BY 2.0/3.0/4.0, CC BY-SA 3.0/4.0 or public-domain status. Preserve each asset's attribution, supplied title/source link and license/version; identify crops or other adaptations and follow its ShareAlike obligations where applicable. APEX uses source-hosted thumbnails and CSS framing, not synthesized faces. Do not replace the asset's license with the combined dataset license.

G2, Fnatic, Liquid, NIP and Virtus.pro marks come from official media/brand resources or a copying policy; they are **not Creative Commons**. Preserve the original proportions, colors and full opacity; no filters, distortions or crest effects. NIP requires clear space of at least one-third of symbol size. Virtus.pro requires an active source link. G2 explicitly addresses media use; Fnatic/Liquid/NIP use follows the stated purpose of their published brand resources rather than a blanket redistribution grant. Exact sources, usage basis and remaining uncertainty are in [Media](media.md). No reusable Vitality, Falcons, FURIA or Luminosity logo is included.

## Excluded material and publication boundary

Machado's CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 archive and row-level comparisons remain local. Public exports must exclude its raw files, detailed reconciliation data and `reconciliation.md`. Aggregate QA agreement counts and bibliographic citations describe the research process; they do not supply Machado observations or feed canonical performance values. Quarantined Ted Tay files and other unselected/restricted datasets are likewise excluded. Neither this data license nor media licenses automatically license the application's software.

The public release is the generated site, its selected documentation, licensed media and analytical derivatives—not the raw-data directory, local database, caches or workspace. The source inventory/media documentation scan found no credentials or machine-specific paths. Asset lookup keys are documented research identifiers; image URLs are public source locations.
