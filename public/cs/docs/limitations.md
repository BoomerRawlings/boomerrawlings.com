# Known limitations

- No demonstrated census. Acquired period 2012-09-13..2021-05-30 and 2023-10-25..2026-11-21; the middle gap, beginning and latest days are absent. Event/region coverage is source-selected. Adequacy for any specific claim must be assessed, not inferred from row counts.
- Professional status follows source tournament catalogs after explicit exhibition/all-star exclusions. Employment, all qualifiers and every mislabeled event cannot be independently certified. Amateur matches may remain in broad competition; reconstructed top30 is a stricter sensitivity cohort.
- Filipe IDs identify mapstats pages, not series. Counts of its match containers are not counts of actual matches. Source IDs cannot prove chronological map order within the same day. Date-level ambiguity can affect the exact start of a consecutive-map peak.
- Ektarr's ID system differs; most cross-source player identities remain deliberately unlinked. Verified anchors do not imply exhaustive identity resolution. Source IDs themselves may contain undocumented changes.
- Team corporate/rebrand history is incomplete. Recent roster lineage IDs approximate continuity and can split organizations or inherit performance through transfers. Elo is based only on acquired matches, does not reconstruct a true world ranking and lacks a pre-archive burn-in history.
- Published historical top30 primary specification unavailable. Do not present reconstructed Elo as HLTV rankings. Source snapshots may contain errors, incomplete scoreboards and source discrepancies.
- Validation used `PRAGMA quick_check` plus separate foreign-key, uniqueness and logical checks; full `PRAGMA integrity_check` did not complete. The executed release passed 21/21 invariants, 811 sampled window-membership/count checks and 34 independently recomputed prior-only reference checks. These establish tested behavior, not complete physical/index integrity or historical truth.
- Canonical validation attrition differs sharply: 99.01% pass for Filipe CS:GO versus 83.48% for Ektarr CS2; aggregate 97.22%. Rates exclude source records never canonicalized and are not coverage estimates. Differential exclusion can change era comparisons.
- Scoreboard rounds assume full participation by five listed players. Demo-derived player participation is unavailable. Suicide/teamkill differences up to five per team-map are tolerated; other conservation failures invalidate the map.
- No verified player birth dates, roles, resources, patches, economy balance, complete map-pool history, LAN/online labels or stage taxonomy. Null does not mean zero. Conditional raw-rate comparability is not equal underlying skill across CS:GO/CS2 or MR15/MR12.
- KPR and Net KPR share kill production; their equal-weight PPI partly counts it twice. Weights, reference period and competition filter can change ranking. PPI/ADR are descriptive, not proven round contribution.
- Prior-year normalization controls aggregation unit/game/filter but not regional missingness or role/team-resource confounding. It includes latest one window/player, equally weighted. Warm-up periods have small reference cohorts; even 20 may be unstable.
- Maxima suffer multiple comparisons, overlapping-window dependence, extreme-value bias and career opportunity differences. Matched observed exposure and conditional event resampling diagnose parts; they do not solve missing-not-at-random historical coverage or provide selection-adjusted true-best probabilities.
- No role/map/stage/resource-adjusted models, opponent-adjusted regression, validated tournament-strength classifier, ADR-inclusive composite or causal round-contribution model was fitted. No maximum-selection resampling or independent future holdout study was executed. A zero median matched-exposure difference does not establish absence of opportunity bias.
- Static website exports top100 per metric/date, monthly snapshots and exact record frontier dates. Full precision/complete windows remain in SQLite. Player trajectories from exported snapshots are explicitly sparse. Inactivity is 90 days since latest qualifying window, not a declaration of retirement.
- Age trajectories and true professional-debut dates remain unavailable. The persistence comparison counts observed exported endpoints; it is not an estimate of continuous time above an elite threshold. Public search cannot expose players absent from every exported top100 snapshot.
- Photos/logos only when reusable source assets are verified. Missing media has labeled fallback. Current photograph does not purport to show appearance at each historical date. Team branding reflects available verified logo, not necessarily a logo from the exact historical year.
- More licensed raw map coverage for mid2021–2023, verified historical ranks, stable cross-source identity bridges, demo-derived participation/roles/resources and official roster-date archives would materially improve the research.
