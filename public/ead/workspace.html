POEMS

Emily Anne

don't feed my ego
you know - I am a leo
she said with a smirk and a grin.

I smirked back
for she thought that was that
not knowing that I was her twin

I have found myself comfortable with silence.
The potential for more, the way that it could expand into anything.

We have spoken of certainty. Of bad news being better than no news.

I think that silence brings with it a certainty. One that most don't hear.

When I am in silence, I have learned to tune my frequency to a quiet sound.

The breath of the universe.

Do you hear it too?

Maybe it's the cosmic microwaves resonating through my bones.
Maybe it's a solar wind adjusting how the atmosphere sits over the earth.
maybe it's the push and pull of the tides, the ocean gaining and receding, shifting it's hold on us.
maybe it's god, whispering in my ear.

I do my best to listen to her. To hear her out. To listen to the wind and move with the water.

The breath in that silence is deafening to me.

But it often feels lonely.

Nobody else hears the storm brewing over the horizon
No one else sees the shape of the stars

But then I met you.

You were always there. Like a planet in the solar system that I somehow missed. Your gravitational pull had been shaping the orbits of every body around it silently.

Deafeningly.
Obviously and unambiguously.

you had been tuned to the same frequency.

You were waiting for someone else to come on the radio.

To know your callsign intuitively.

To introduce themselves one moment and have lived a million lifetimes the next.

A sense of belonging.

A blanket wrapped around you without the burden of entanglement,

a shot in the arm, self inflicted
a shot to your throat, the familiar but aged memory rushing back
a shot to the mind, like there was a moment before, when life existed, and a moment after like something just paused.

But the pause is perceived.

It's not real.

It's just you and me

It's a cold plunge.
Sand in my mouth

It's burning matchbooks whole. To watch the burn, but to keep us warm.
It's the distance between us. Inches felt like miles.

It's a sense of peace to a sense of dread within moments.

it's false certainty in some things and true doubts in others.

But regardless.

Here we are.

Locked in orbit.

I don't know if this was my doing or yours. I don't know if god nudged us closer or the universe swept us into eachother.

You know. We both have said it, and I think we both meant it.

There are millions more of me and there are millions more of you.

How foolish,
For we know that's not true.

A lie said to the other,
But really for me.

Whatever we do,
Whatever may be,
There's only one of you,
And only one of me.

SCOPE & METHOD

What this briefing covers

The atlas connects public research across agentic AI, security and social science. Contact cards identify their sources; solid lines mark documented relationships and dashed lines mark inferred connections. The paper catalogue retains the original Yue Zhang and collaborator literature collection. Research sources catalogue the expanded institutional index; reported cases retain their original attribution and evidence status.

Findings report the papers’ results. Anomalies identify measurement or provenance issues. Design proposals are our applications of the evidence, not experimentally validated systems.

Search and graphs run locally in the browser. No analytics, remote fonts, or automatic third-party requests are added. Primary sources open only when selected.
