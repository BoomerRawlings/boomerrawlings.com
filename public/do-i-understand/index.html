Do I Understand?

Can I explain it without the AI? And can I still use it when the problem changes?

Download SKILL.mdView on GitHub

What it does

  1. Explain it. Describe how or why it works in your own words.
  2. Try a new case. Predict what happens when one important condition changes.
  3. See the gap. Get feedback on what your answers actually showed and what they didn't.

Try it in your project

Save the same file at the path for your client:

  • Codex: .agents/skills/do-i-understand/SKILL.md
  • Claude Code: .claude/skills/do-i-understand/SKILL.md

Run $do-i-understand in Codex or /do-i-understand in Claude Code, followed by what you want to check. Or paste the instructions into a fresh chat to try it without installing.

What I’m testing

Does a question without hints, followed by a new case, reveal a gap I didn’t know I had? Before starting, predict where you’ll get stuck. Afterward, note what surprised you, whether the model gave away the answer, and whether its assessment matched what you actually said. See the test instructions.

An early prototype, not a validated test of mastery or originality. The skill doesn't collect results.
